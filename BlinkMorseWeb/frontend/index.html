About the Project

Bridging Communication Gaps

Blink Morse AI is a web-based assistive communication system designed for patients with speech impairments, caregivers, and anyone who needs an alternative way to communicate.

🎯

The Problem

Millions of people with ALS, locked-in syndrome, or other conditions lose the ability to speak. Traditional AAC devices are expensive and complex.

💡

Our Solution

Using just a webcam and a browser, patients can communicate by blinking Morse code patterns that are decoded into text and spoken aloud by AI.

🔒

Privacy First

All face detection and blink processing happens locally in your browser. No video data is ever uploaded or stored. Only decoded text is sent for speech synthesis.
